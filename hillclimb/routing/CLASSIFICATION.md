# Geometric triage evidence

Historical `*.contact-audit.json` files contain **proposals**, with input, producer
and audit hashes. The owner subsequently sanctioned Visible for contact within
model tolerance (`tieConvention`) and independent exact decisions where the full
native reference is Unresolved/Unset (`exactCheckDecided`). The final compiled gate
regenerates per-sample evidence against its frozen producer; it does not accept
the historical proposals by association. Native definite results remain strict
outside a certified contact or the existing native-order control.
Every coincident kernel contributor vetoes that exception, including a contributor
below the winning class. The harness checks that veto with a synthetic control.

## Planar uncut and mixed M/L

all-pairs oracle agree with the routed output in both directions, including the
instanced boxes and coordinate/unit fixtures. The oracle reports every edge/face
pair count, intervals, loci and producer/input hashes.

accounts for all 251 samples as historical contact proposals. These are not
native-order exceptions. The final gate must independently certify each sample
under the owner's contact rule before accepting it.

Mixed scenes still have unresolved native duplicate ownership and native-only

retained six-unit support. Inverse-frame roundoff skews the short proxy enough for

Affine positive-weight line overlap now checks the actual overlap, and depth
certification rejects a hidden alias behind the candidate by half model tolerance.
This reduces failures, but does not establish complete nonlinear duplicate coverage.

## Exact mixed reference and boundary certificates

For a native Unresolved/Unset sample, the independent reference reconstructs the
contributing candidates and checks every planar face, with rational predicates.
Curved occlusion needs a definite native query on the same world support or
exclusion by all conservative curved-body bounds. A planar Hidden proof can
decide the occluder union; Visible needs a definite curved decision as well.
Unknown curved coverage stays unexplained. The evidence records candidates,
face IDs, signed gaps, curved proofs and complete pair counts in
`*.exact-witnesses.jsonl`. `All(Eligible)` and the raw kernel-footprint veto remain.

Some rounded publication endpoints put a scalar probe just beyond the strict
depth threshold. A separate full-interval all-pairs check can certify a Visible
interval within the unchanged comparison-distance tolerance. Each certificate
binds its producer and every input hash, checks an interior Visible probe, and
requires definite curved coverage in mixed scenes. These count as
`toleranceArtefact`, separately from contact and native uncertainty decisions.
`routing_verify.py` preserves raw compiled rows and writes the hash-bound
`verified-gate.json`; an uncertified contradiction remains a failure.

## Deliberate limits

No arbitrary radius, widened model tolerance, native-subset disagreement or
unproven duplicate has been promoted to confidence. Positive planar/contact
evidence is required per sample. Native uncertainty without an independent
decision, and nonlinear duplicate ownership without complete attribution, still
fail. Zero bugs or merge readiness cannot be inferred from the speed improvement.

The owner's final branch gate accepts Unresolved/Info audit verdicts for residual
uncertified comparisons, while requiring zero known bugs and zero evidenced
wrong-Visible samples in small fixtures and M. Two original unknown block-instance
cases now have complete independent planar agreement; the six other small cases
and all eight M views retain Info. The raw strict whole-output verdict is preserved.
Large gaps are non-blocking for this branch and listed in HILLCLIMB.md; the full
233-view gate remains mandatory before public beta.
